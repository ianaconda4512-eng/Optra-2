The Problem

African creators are building audiences.
But they can't get paid.

Every tool built for creator monetization was designed for the West. PayPal blocks African accounts. Stripe isn't available in most African countries. Gumroad restricts African sellers. The gap is massive — and nobody has fixed it. Until now.

💳

Payment Rails Don't Work

Stripe and PayPal are unavailable or unreliable across most of Africa. Creators are left out of the global creator economy.

🔗

Too Many Fragmented Tools

Linktree for links. WhatsApp for orders. MoMo for payments. Google Forms for bookings. It's chaos. Creators need one home.

🌍

Nothing Built For Africa

Every creator tool assumes a Western user. African creators are an afterthought — patched in, never prioritized.

📦

No Digital Product Infrastructure

Selling presets, templates, eBooks, or exclusive content? African creators have nowhere reliable to list, sell, and deliver.
